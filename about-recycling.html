Our Story

From a small garage to a city-wide mission

Every year we've grown — not just in numbers, but in the depth of our commitment to cleaner cities.

2019 · The Spark

Where it all began

Three friends, one tiny pickup truck, and a frustration with how hard it was to recycle properly. We started collecting waste from 40 nearby homes.

2021 · Scaling Up

From homes to neighborhoods

We crossed 500 household signups across 3 cities and launched our first business recycling program for small offices and cafés.

2023 · Going Digital

Green Points & the app

We launched the Recycling Service platform — real-time pickup tracking, Green Points rewards, and a dashboard for businesses to monitor their impact.

2026 · Today

85 cities and growing

Today we serve households and businesses across 85 cities, with a network of certified processing partners and a team that's as passionate as day one.

What drives us, every single day

Two ideas that shape every pickup, every process, every decision we make.

01

Our Mission

Make responsible recycling effortless for every home and business — by combining reliable doorstep pickup with transparent, verifiable processing.

  • Free doorstep pickup for every household
  • Certified processing with zero landfill diversion
  • Real-time tracking and Green Points rewards
  • Educational programs for schools and RWAs
02

Our Vision

A future where recycling is a natural habit — not a chore — and where every city treats waste as a resource, not a problem.

  • Zero-waste cities powered by community recycling
  • A circular economy for plastics, paper, and metals
  • Every school teaching recycling from day one
  • Carbon-neutral logistics across our fleet
