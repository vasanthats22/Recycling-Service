Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often from households and businesses.

Scheduling a pickup is simple — just sign in to your account, go to My Pickups, and choose a date and time slot that works for you. You'll receive an SMS confirmation and a reminder one hour before the truck arrives. You can also call our support team directly.
We accept plastics (PET, HDPE, PP), paper and cardboard, metals (aluminium, steel, copper), e-waste (laptops, phones, batteries), glass, and organic waste. Hazardous materials like paints, chemicals, and medical waste are handled through our specialised disposal partners — please contact us for details.
Standard household pickups are free of charge. In fact, you earn Green Points for every kilogram recycled, which you can redeem for eco-friendly rewards. Special pickups (like large e-waste) may incur a small handling fee.
Absolutely. Our Business Solutions team handles commercial-scale collections — from warehouses, offices, hotels, and malls. We provide custom pickup schedules, dedicated account managers, monthly sustainability reports, and ESG-compliant documentation.
All e-waste goes through certified data destruction using DoD 5220.22-M standards. We provide you with a Certificate of Destruction for every device. Hard drives are physically shredded, and you receive photographic evidence on request.
